# Code of Conduct

Eggshell welcomes people who want to improve the project in good faith.

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, and assume that unclear
wording is a chance to ask a question. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project
spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the project and its community. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, private where possible, and based on the behavior rather than
the contributor's identity.

For an ordinary disagreement, use the relevant issue or pull request. For a
sensitive report, contact the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting
channel rather than posting personal information publicly. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
